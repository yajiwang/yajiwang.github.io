I am a computational social scientist working at the intersection of large-scale social media data and large language models.

I combine behavioral data from X (Twitter) with bibliometric, citation, and altmetric databases to study how knowledge is produced, how it spreads, and who gets left out. My current work is centred on the empirical evaluation of LLMs in social research: comparing LLM and human reference selection, measuring gender bias in LLM-driven knowledge production, and mapping the limits of LLMs for name-based inference.

I am part of the Computational Inequalities Research Group, funded by the European Union under the Horizon EU project LearnData (101086712). Before Budapest I completed my PhD in Science of Science and Management of Science and Technology at Dalian University of Technology, with a visiting period at the University of Copenhagen supported by the China Scholarship Council.

Methods: machine learning, network science, text-as-data, statistical modelling. Tools: Python, R, SQL, PyTorch, the Hugging Face ecosystem.
